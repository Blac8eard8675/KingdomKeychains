MY RESURRECTION STORY

From Fear to Faith • From Brokenness to Purpose

Victoria Schwab

Master Craftsman

Owner, Kingdom Keychains

About Me

Good Morning

First, I give God all the honor, glory, and praise for allowing me to be here today. If it weren't for God, I would not be the person I am today. I thank God for keeping me alive, restoring me, and showing me that He must be first in my life.

My Faith Journey

Pastor Kevin and Lady Donna, thank you for everything you have poured into my life. Through Restricted No More, I learned that I cannot keep pouring from a broken vessel without God at the center. God has been teaching me how to heal, trust Him, and understand my worth.

Faith Over Fear

Mary Magdalene reminds me that a person's past does not have to become their identity. I know what it is like to be broken, afraid, and feel trapped by the past. But God showed me that my past is part of my testimony—it is not who I am.

Like Peter, there were times when I looked at the storm and began to sink. But when I cried out to Jesus, He reached for me. My faith may have been as small as a mustard seed, but God was always bigger than my fear.

Who I Am Today

2 Timothy 1:7 reminds me that God has not given me a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind. Today I am choosing faith over fear, purpose over my past, and God over everything.

My Resurrection

Kingdom Keychains represents something important to me: not the person I used to be, but the person God is making me today.

I am not my abuse. I am not my fear. I am not my mistakes. I am a child of God.

I am redeemed. I am restored. I am still standing. And I am walking forward in faith.

Thank you, Pastor Kevin and Lady Donna, for helping me discover that my past does not determine my future. Most of all, thank You, God, for giving me a new life and a new purpose.

THIS IS NOT WHO I WAS.
THIS IS WHO GOD IS MAKING ME.
