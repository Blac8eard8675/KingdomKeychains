About Me

Welcome to Kingdom Keychains! My name is Victoria Schwab, and I give God all the honor, glory, and praise for bringing me to where I am today. He has kept me, restored me, and taught me that no matter what I have faced, He must always come first in my life.

Through my faith journey and the support of Pastor Kevin and Lady Donna, I have learned that my past does not define me. Through Restricted No More, God has been teaching me to heal, trust Him, and walk in the purpose He has placed on my life. I am learning to choose faith over fear and to trust that God can turn my brokenness into a testimony of His goodness, mercy, and grace.

The Heart Behind Kingdom Keychains

Kingdom Keychains is more than a business to me. Every handmade keychain represents a piece of my faith and reminds me of the God who has carried me through life's storms.

Crosses: Represent God as our Anchor, reminding us to remain grounded in Him through every storm.

Ropes: Represent Jesus Christ as our connection to God, reminding us that through Him, we can draw near to our Heavenly Father.

Chain Links: Represent the disciples and their connection to Jesus Christ and God, reminding us of the importance of faith, unity, and sharing Christ's love with others.

My journey has not always been easy, but God continues to remind me that redemption is real, restoration is possible, and my past does not determine my future.

I am not my abuse. I am not my fear. I am not my mistakes. I am a child of God. I am redeemed. I am being restored. I am still standing. And I am moving forward in faith.

Kingdom Keychains is a reflection of who God is making me today, not who I used to be. My prayer is that every keychain serves as a reminder that no matter what you have been through, there is always hope in Jesus Christ. God can bring beauty out of brokenness and lead us into a future filled with purpose.

Thank you for visiting Kingdom Keychains and supporting my small business. Every bit of support means so much to me!

THIS IS NOT WHO I WAS.

THIS IS WHO GOD IS MAKING ME.

All honor, glory, and praise belong to God. May God bless you!
